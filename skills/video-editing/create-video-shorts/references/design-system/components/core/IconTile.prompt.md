IconTile is a glossy, slightly tilted 3D tile with a white glyph: the personality of a slide.

```jsx
<IconTile color="azure" glyph="code" size={116} tilt={-10} float />
<IconTile color="iris" glyph="eyes" size={100} tilt={9} float style={{ animationDelay: "-3s" }} />
```

Size 96–120 beside a title. Alternate the tilt sign when two sit together, and stagger floats with a negative `animationDelay`. One or two per slide: above a hero title, after the first word of a two-line title, or over the top-right corner of the winning panel. Never a row of four, never in the webcam lane.
