TaskCard is a white card floating on the stage, with a small glossy tile, a title and one line of description.

```jsx
<TaskCard color="ember" glyph="arrow" title="Route to billing" description="Confidence 0.81 lets your code act or escalate" float />
```

Overlap one card on the edge of a ProductWindow or screenshot, or stack two or three with a 40–50px offset. 450px wide by default. Never a grid of cards, never in the webcam lane.
