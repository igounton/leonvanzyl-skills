ChatBubble is one chat message inside a ProductWindow.

```jsx
<ProductWindow tabs={["Chat", "Computer", "Activity"]} active="Chat" badge="gpt-6 astra">
  <ChatBubble name="Iggy · your dot">You haven’t invoiced the September piece yet. I’ve drafted it.</ChatBubble>
  <ChatBubble from="you">Looks right. Send it.</ChatBubble>
</ProductWindow>
```

Two to four bubbles, real-sounding and short. Only inside paper, never directly on the stage.
